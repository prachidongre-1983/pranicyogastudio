Meet your instructor

Prachi Rajmane

Certified Yoga Teacher · YTT-200
PhD in Molecular Biology & Immunology

I bring over nine years of personal yoga practice and an extensive scientific research background to my teaching. At Pranic Yoga Studio, I draw on Patanjali’s yoga principles and modern scientific insights to make yoga approachable and meaningful.

My goal is to help you understand your practice, move with awareness, and build a regular space for physical and mental wellbeing. Classes combine asanas, breathwork, and relaxation, with personal attention in a small-group setting.
